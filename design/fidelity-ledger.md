  final code changes. All public content and artwork URLs derive from Vite's base.
- An actual-source Node smoke check passed for canonical pet URLs, supported
  locales, origin rejection, invalid and future verification dates, publication
  gating, search and role filtering.
- The desktop and QR captures show the unverified-runtime state. They do not prove
  authentication, adoption, provider execution or persistent conversation.

  the coordinator's clipboard read. The final code adds explicit live success

  message addition postdates the stored desktop QR image and was build-checked.

  layout, Aiven selection and the Aiven QR dialog successfully. The coordinator
  measured `body.scrollWidth === documentElement.clientWidth === 375`; the
  390 px frame includes a scrollbar. No horizontal overflow was observed.
- The UI worker inspected both narrow-layout screenshots. The title wraps
  naturally, controls remain visible, the selected pet art is correctly placed,
  and the full QR dialog fits within the frame. The main page scrolls vertically
  as expected to reveal the remaining profile content.
- This was a responsive desktop-browser check, not a physical phone, mobile
  Safari or mobile Chrome test. Touch and device-specific behavior remain untested.
- The browser download wait timed out with a kernel reset. No completed browser
  download is claimed; the download controls and generated QR are visible, but
  a saved file was not verified.
- The coordinator owns exact QR decoding, final integrated browser checks and
