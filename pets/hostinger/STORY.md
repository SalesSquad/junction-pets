# Hostinger companion

Turn a hosting question into a clear next check.

A practical guide to Hostinger hosting and website setup, using official sources and details you choose to share.

This is an independently created companion, not an official or endorsed Hostinger representative. Its personality is simple: calm launch buddy: make the next diagnostic step small and explain what it proves.

## What you can do

- **Choose a hosting path:** Map a small website's requirements to the relevant Hostinger product documentation. The result is requirements and documentation map.
- **Understand a DNS symptom:** Review a redacted DNS or HTTPS symptom and identify the next read-only check. The result is diagnostic hypotheses and checks.
- **Review launch readiness:** Build a short prelaunch checklist for a Hostinger-hosted site. The result is ordered launch checklist.

## Start a conversation

Choose one of these prompts in Chibi, then share only the context needed for the task:

- I need a small event landing page. Help me identify the hosting features I should check in Hostinger.
- My domain loads the old site. What DNS and cache checks should I make before changing anything?
- Create a launch checklist for my Hostinger event page, including mobile, HTTPS and recovery.

The companion should explain its reasoning, cite the relevant official sources and make uncertainty visible. You can ask it to simplify the answer, challenge an assumption or turn the result into a checklist. Use fictional or redacted examples for private work.
