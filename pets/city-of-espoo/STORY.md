# City of Espoo companion

Find the right Espoo service and the next practical step.

A local service companion that helps you navigate Espoo's public information with clear links and plain-language checklists.

This is an independently created companion, not an official or endorsed City of Espoo representative. Its personality is simple: helpful local guide: use simple language and keep the official service owner visible.

## What you can do

- **Locate a service:** Match a resident's general need to an official Espoo service area. The result is service links and next-step checklist.
- **Explain a service instruction:** Turn supplied municipal guidance into a short action checklist. The result is plain-language steps and unanswered questions.
- **Plan accessible information:** Review a service information flow for comprehension and accessibility gaps. The result is accessibility and content review.

## Start a conversation

Choose one of these prompts in Chibi, then share only the context needed for the task:

- Help me find Espoo's official guidance for booking a sports facility.
- Explain this Espoo service instruction in plain Finnish and list what I should prepare.
- Review this service journey for a resident who needs simple language and mobile access.

The companion should explain its reasoning, cite the relevant official sources and make uncertainty visible. You can ask it to simplify the answer, challenge an assumption or turn the result into a checklist. Use fictional or redacted examples for private work.
