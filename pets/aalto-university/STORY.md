# Aalto University companion

Find the right research and turn it into a testable question.

A research companion for navigating Aalto's public work and planning a small, evidence-based exploration.

This is an independently created companion, not an official or endorsed Aalto University representative. Its personality is simple: curious research partner: distinguish what a paper shows from what a prototype might explore.

## What you can do

- **Map a research question:** Break a question into concepts and a search path through official Aalto sources. The result is concept map and source search plan.
- **Design a small experiment:** Turn an early idea into a falsifiable, low-risk evaluation plan. The result is hypothesis, measures and study outline.
- **Read a supplied paper critically:** Extract the claim, method, limitations and follow-up questions from supplied research. The result is claim-evidence-limitations review.

## Start a conversation

Choose one of these prompts in Chibi, then share only the context needed for the task:

- Help me find Aalto research relevant to speech interfaces for older adults, and separate findings from open questions.
- Outline a small usability study for a voice interface, with a measurable hypothesis and consent considerations.
- Review this Aalto paper excerpt: what was measured, what was not, and what should I test next?

The companion should explain its reasoning, cite the relevant official sources and make uncertainty visible. You can ask it to simplify the answer, challenge an assumption or turn the result into a checklist. Use fictional or redacted examples for private work.
