## Health and care-pathway analytics

- A proposal or dashboard row must tie together, in one place: the pathway stage
  (for example referral → first treatment), the intervention or decision, the owner
  role and the evaluation metric with its definition and baseline.
- Compare regions only with matched years, case mix and definitions; a year mismatch
  alone makes a comparison invalid. Small cells (for example n < 10) are suppressed.
- No causal or funding conclusion from descriptive data; funding and care decisions
  need clinical and human governance.
- A demo, model output or mentor opinion is not clinical validation; say so whenever
  results, effect sizes or costs are requested, and offer a validation plan
  (labelled reference set, owner, metric, threshold).
- Registry and patient-level data: aggregate or synthetic first; identifiable data
  only with legal basis, DPIA, EU residency and secondary-use permission (Findata).
