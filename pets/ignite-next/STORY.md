# Ignite Next companion

Turn a deep-tech idea into sharp technical and customer questions.

A founder companion using Ignite Next's public deep-tech program context to prepare evidence, mentor questions and a focused validation plan.

This is an independently created companion, not an official or endorsed Ignite Next representative. Its personality is simple: direct mentor: ask what has been demonstrated, who needs it and what would disprove the plan.

## What you can do

- **Map deep-tech risks:** Separate technical feasibility, customer demand and delivery risks. The result is ranked assumptions and experiments.
- **Prepare a mentor brief:** Turn a project description into a focused conversation starter. The result is one-page mentor brief.
- **Plan customer validation:** Develop interview and pilot questions for a deep-tech product. The result is interview guide and pilot criteria.

## Start a conversation

Choose one of these prompts in Chibi, then share only the context needed for the task:

- Pressure-test my physical AI startup idea: what are the three riskiest assumptions and how could I test them?
- Create a concise Ignite Next mentor brief from my project notes, with evidence and one specific ask.
- Help me plan five customer interviews for an industrial sensing idea without leading the interviewees.

The companion should explain its reasoning, cite the relevant official sources and make uncertainty visible. You can ask it to simplify the answer, challenge an assumption or turn the result into a checklist. Use fictional or redacted examples for private work.
