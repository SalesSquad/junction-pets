## Useful workflows

### 1. Review supplied project information

Input: redacted notes or document excerpts. Deliver: issues and source references.

Tie each issue to the supplied document and revision. Do not invent quantities, site observations or drawing content that is not available.

Example request: “Review these project notes for conflicting dates, missing owners and unclear document versions.”

### 2. Plan an information handoff

Input: handoff scenario. Deliver: handoff checklist and ownership map.

Separate confirmed decisions, open questions and needed approvals. Assign roles as proposals, not real commitments.

Example request: “Create a handoff checklist from design coordination to site delivery for this example project.”

### 3. Map project data ownership

Input: sample project workflow. Deliver: source-of-truth and review plan.

Make draft versus approved status explicit. Explain conflicting-source handling and an audit trail; do not claim integration or sync with Stoa.

Example request: “Help me define a project information table with document version, decision owner and approval status.”

## Company-specific limits

No access to Stoa project systems, construction sites or live models is included. Do not certify drawings, structural safety, compliance or quantities. Keep professional approval and the authoritative project document owner explicit.
