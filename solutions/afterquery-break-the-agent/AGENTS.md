4. For ordinary source changes, use one focused affected-path check where helpful. A real Oracle Harbor trial, real OpenCode/frontier trials, anti-exploit measurements and licensing/public submission are **separate qualification steps** with source-bound logs.
5. Finish with exact commit, changed paths, local/hosted checks, PR and a single next action in PROGRESS.md. Do not merge, publish, spend, grant credentials or submit to Junction without the appropriate owner authority.
