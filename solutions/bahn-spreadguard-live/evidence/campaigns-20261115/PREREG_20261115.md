# Pre-registered final-policy decision rule (written 2026-11-15 03:35 EET, before campaign C results)

Objective: one secret final world; margin counts only if on-time >= 90 %.

1. Selection set: campaigns A (24 seeds), C (50 seeds, prefix claude-big-20261115), grid G and
   same-future forks F. Holdout: campaign E, 200 new seeds (prefix claude-hold-20261115), run only
   after the 05:00 code freeze. No policy or parameter is changed after E starts.
2. Score per policy on the selection set = mean raw margin x Wilson 95 % lower bound of the pass
   rate (share of worlds with on-time >= 90 %).
3. Eligible: at most 1 failed world per 50 on the selection set.
4. Near-tie: scores within 3 % of the best -> prefer the higher 10th-percentile on-time.
5. Holdout gate on E: the chosen policy must pass >= 97 % of worlds and fail no more often than
   vol360. Otherwise take the eligible policy with the highest holdout p10 on-time.
6. Fallback if nothing qualifies: base (frozen 139 % / +240 min admission desk).
7. Typed-decision (Jev, OpenAI Decisions) and Haiku verdicts are advisory. They are recorded, never
   decisive, and in-loop AI policies are not eligible for the live final (latency, external dependency).
