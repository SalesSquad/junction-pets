Measure the mistakes.

An experimental graph screen learned from real campaign and organic trend annotations. It examines interaction topology; it cannot establish deception, bot identity or intent.

Loading evaluation…

LEN-small contains 100 Turkish Twitter trend engagement graphs. Each complete trend stays in one partition. Training uses only twelve numeric graph features; the threshold is selected using calibration data with an empirical false-alarm target of at most 10%.

These results apply to this small dataset. They do not measure the post coordination lab, live feeds, other languages or production accuracy.

Keep the comparison honest.

Size alone can predict dataset selection. The size-only baseline exposes that shortcut. Scores are uncalibrated; graphs and recurring actors may overlap across partitions.
