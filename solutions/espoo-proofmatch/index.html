How the score works, and where it falls short

For each required skill, take the strongest eligible card: artifact 0.90, walkthrough 0.70, statement 0.20. A concrete non-empty outcome adds 0.10 (maximum 1.00). Cards older than 180 days receive ×0.80; future-dated cards are ignored. Multiply each skill’s support by its share of the role’s weights, sum, then round to one decimal out of 100. Ties sort by stable candidate ID. Bios, names, availability and personal characteristics do not affect this score.

Source form and outcomes are self-declared heuristics. They do not prove authenticity, originality, proficiency or who wrote the material. A fabricated artifact could fool this demo. No AI-text detection is performed. Lack of evidence may reflect unequal access to portfolios; keep an open conversation and offer another way to demonstrate skills.

The keyword baseline counts exact skill-ID tokens in the bio. This tiny, intentionally adversarial dataset is an explanation exercise, not an independent study or evidence of fair or effective hiring.
