# Cascade at inference: does a judge let more notes be pre-filled for one-click confirmation?

Generated by `scribe/v3/cascade.py --resolve "off" analyse`; every number is in `data/scribe/v3/cascade/cascade-v1.json`. All notes are SYNTHETIC (simulated pathways, model-written notes). Nothing here is for clinical use or shows patient benefit, and a judge is a language model that gives a second opinion, not the truth.

The idea: the student proposes events and a person confirms every one. A note may be pre-filled for one-click confirmation only if the error among pre-filled notes stays at a target; every other note needs editing. The thresholds below were chosen on validation notes and are empirical, not guarantees: on test they can, and here sometimes did, give a higher error than the target.

## How it was measured

- Student: **V3-270m-nemotron**, validation exact match 124/310. Highest validation exact match is C-270m-all-v2data (134/310); the pre-registered primary V3-270m-nemotron (124/310) and it lie within each other's Wilson 95% intervals, so the rule prefers the primary. Candidates on validation: C-270m-all-v2data 134/310; S-1b-lora-r16 125/310; V3-270m-all 125/310; V3-270m-nemotron 124/310; v2-selected 112/310. Test results played no part in the choice.
- Signals: student confidence = mean token log-probability of the student's answer (higher = more confident); Jev score = judge score from Jev: min over predicted events of P(event stated) x (1 - P(unlisted event)); 0 when Jev's route choice differs from the student's route (question set v2); student + Jev = ridge logistic regression of exact match on (student, jev), fitted on validation notes.
- Decisions: 93 validation and 93 test notes, order sha256('scribe-v3-cascade-decisions|' + id) ascending, within validation and within the pooled test notes; size rule: largest K <= 120 per split whose estimated tokens stay <= 270000 (90% of the 300000 cap).
- Threshold: on validation notes only: accept a note when its score >= t; t is the value with the largest validation coverage whose observed error among accepted notes is <= the target, with at least 20 accepted notes; otherwise no threshold is set.
- Notes whose prediction did not parse are not sent to a judge and are never accepted by any signal; coverage counts them in its denominator. Leak guard: assert_validation_only() raises before any fit or threshold sees a note whose split is not 'val' or whose id is a test id; unit-tested in scribe/v3/tests/test_cascade.py.
- Intervals: Wilson 95% for shares; paired percentile bootstrap over test notes, 2000 resamples, seed '20261114|<section>|<set>'; thresholds held fixed, so it omits the noise of choosing them (see pipeline_bootstrap). The pipeline bootstrap resamples validation and test notes together and re-chooses thresholds each time.

## Limits

- Synthetic notes with model-written labels; the outcome is exact match of the whole answer, and the person who reviews is not modelled.
- One student and 310 validation notes (250 Nemotron-written, 60 Haiku-written); the test mix is 500 and 200, so the pooled test mix differs from validation. The thresholds were chosen in-sample on validation.
- The Decisions part rests on a small subset, and its thresholds on even fewer validation notes.
