What this tool does not claim

  • No causal or patient-level inference from county comparisons.
  • No measured mortality benefit from a proposed portfolio.
  • No publicly measured cancer-specific diagnostic or treatment waits.
  • No company endorsement, clinical instruction or implementation approval.
  • No result from simulated people or simulated notes is a finding about Finnish patients; those sections are marked SYNTHETIC.
  • No variant classification and no treatment eligibility from the genomics benchmark; that section is marked RESEARCH.
