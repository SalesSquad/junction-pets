**Narration.** The current track is a synthetic voice (ElevenLabs), made by `python3 tools/voiceover.py`; the in/out times below are where each line is actually spoken. To record a human voice instead:
**How to record.** Read each line so it starts at its "in" time and is finished by its "out" time
(a slightly faster read is fine; never run past "out", or the voice drifts from the captions). Record
one take as mono WAV, 48 kHz, and put it in that slot.

**How to put the voice in.** Either:

1. Replace `assets/voiceover-slot.m4a` with the recording (same file name, 119 s long, encoded as
   AAC), then re-render: `npx --yes hyperframes@0.7.28 render . --quality high --workers 2 --output SAMA-video.mp4`; or
2. Keep the picture and swap only the audio (no re-render, takes seconds):

```bash
ffmpeg -i SAMA-video.mp4 -i voiceover.wav -map 0:v -map 1:a -c:v copy -c:a aac -b:a 160k \
  -af apad -t 119 SAMA-video-voiced.mp4
```

Captions stay burned in either way, so the video also works with the sound off.
